WebbOS Wallpapers Directory
============================

Place your wallpaper images here in BMP or PPM format.

Supported formats:
- BMP: 24-bit or 32-bit uncompressed
- PPM: P6 (binary) format

The system will look for files in this order:
1. default.bmp
2. default.ppm

Recommended resolution: 1280x800 (or any 16:10 aspect ratio)
